# Power BI Dashboard & Business Insights

## Overview

Power BI was used to transform the processed retail data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

The dashboard provides a high-level view of sales and inventory performance while allowing users to explore trends and identify areas requiring attention.

## Key Dashboard Metrics

The dashboard includes key performance indicators such as:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Inventory levels
- Sales trends

## Sales Analysis

The dashboard provides visibility into sales performance over time.

Users can analyse revenue trends and identify periods of higher or lower sales activity.

## Revenue by Location

Revenue can be analysed by city or geographic location.

This helps identify locations that are contributing the most to overall revenue.

## Product Performance

Product-level analysis allows the business to identify products that are performing well based on sales activity.

This information can support decisions around product availability, promotions and inventory planning.

## Inventory Analysis

Inventory information provides visibility into available stock.

The dashboard can be used to identify products with lower stock levels and support better inventory management.

## Average Order Value

Average Order Value (AOV) provides an indication of the average amount spent per order.

This metric can help the business understand purchasing behaviour and monitor changes in customer spending.

## Business Value

The dashboard converts raw operational data into information that can support business decisions.

The solution can help management:

- Monitor overall performance
- Identify sales trends
- Compare geographic performance
- Monitor product demand
- Identify inventory concerns
- Make data-driven decisions

## Reporting Technology

The dashboard was developed using:

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- SQL
- Data modelling

The report was designed to provide a clear and interactive view of the business data.
